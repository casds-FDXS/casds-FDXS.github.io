01 / About

Building with both the system and its use in mind.

I am an AI application developer with a background in Educational Technology. My work focuses on building experimental tools for memory, learning, knowledge organization, and collaboration.

I tend to approach projects with curiosity, patience, and steady iteration rather than quick conclusions. I am interested not only in whether a technical system works, but also in how people understand it, use it, and adapt it in real situations.

My current work explores long-term human–AI interaction, personal knowledge systems, collaborative learning, and interactive educational applications.

04 / Working Style

Clarify, test, compare, and improve.

I value clear reasoning, careful verification, responsible use of AI, and systems that can be tested in real situations.

My projects often begin with an incomplete idea rather than a fixed specification. I prefer to clarify the problem through prototypes, records, comparison, and gradual iteration.

I am comfortable using AI as a development and production tool, but I also care about understanding what it produces, checking important decisions, and keeping project ownership and responsibility clear.
